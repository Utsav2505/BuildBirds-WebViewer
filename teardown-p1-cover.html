VIKRAM–1
India's next leap into space.
full teardown inside →

On 18 July 2026, Vikram-1 became India's first private rocket to reach orbit — proof that an Indian company can design, build and fly an orbital rocket end-to-end from Indian soil. It made India only the third country, after the US and China, with private orbital launch capability.
