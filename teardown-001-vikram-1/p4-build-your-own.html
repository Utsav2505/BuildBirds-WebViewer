It flew badly? Good — that's data. Read it:
IT WOBBLES & TUMBLES
Unstable. Fins too small, or too far forward. Bigger fins, right at the tail.
IT DROPS LIKE A STONE
Too heavy up front. Take the paperclip off and fly it again.
IT GOES FAR, NOT LONG
Launch angle too flat. You want hang time — aim steeper.
