Why drop a stage?
PROPELLANT BURNS→ EMPTY STAGE = DEAD WEIGHT→ DROP IT→ LIGHTER & FASTER ↑
Hauling empty stages wastes thrust — so the rocket sheds them mid-flight. That's why it's built in stages. (P1 answer: most of a rocket is propellant.)
Think like an engineer
Don't carry what you don't need — every kilo of dead weight is speed you lose.
