Not the printer in your school lab

Now we print metal.

No plastic string. A laser melts metal powder — steel, titanium, nickel superalloy — into a layer 20–60 microns thick, thinner than your hair. Repeat a few thousand times and a solid part grows out of a tray of dust.

POWDER BED
Laser scans a flat bed of powder. Fine detail — injectors, cooling channels.
BLOWN POWDER · DED
Powder sprayed into the beam. Big shapes, and repairing worn parts.
