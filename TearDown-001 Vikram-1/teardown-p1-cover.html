MISSION 001
India's next leap into space

VIKRAM–1

Built by Skyroot Aerospace·Hyderabad, India

On 18 July 2026, it lifted off from Sriharikota and became India's first private rocket to reach orbit — carrying four small satellites to about 450 km up.

Why should I care?
  • 01A company did this, not a space agency. Two ex-ISRO engineers, eight years, one shot.
  • 02One rocket = many brutally hard systems that all have to work, in the right order, once.
  • 03Its last-stage engine is 3D printed in one piece — and can restart itself in space.
Named after Dr. Vikram Sarabhai, the physicist who started India's space programme in 1962.
Crazy fact
To reach orbit, a rocket can't just go up — it has to fly sideways at nearly 28,000 km/h.
That's about 7.8 km every second. Delhi to Mumbai in under 3 minutes.
